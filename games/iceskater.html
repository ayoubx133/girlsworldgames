Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Dress your skater, then spin and jump to the music on the ice!

About the game

Every ice show has a theme card with wishes like a blue skating dress, pink skates or fluffy earmuffs. Choose hairstyles, skating dresses, skates, earmuffs and scarves, then press SKATE. Your skater glides around the rink to a graceful waltz and glowing rings show where she is heading: tap when she reaches one to spin, jump and twirl. The animal judges score your style and your skating, and coins open new sparkle trails.

  • Glittery skating dresses, skates, earmuffs and scarves
  • Tap with the music to spin, jump and twirl
  • An ice rink and six sparkle trails to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
