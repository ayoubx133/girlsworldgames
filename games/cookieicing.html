Sugar Cookie Bakery Chef

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pipe, flood and decorate sugar cookies for bakery orders!

About the game

Choose a cookie shape and trace its outline with the piping bag. Fill it with smooth flood icing and see it settle shiny and glossy. Drop dots of a second color onto the wet icing and pull through them to make tiny hearts, then add pearls or sprinkles. Finish every order and wrap your cookie in a pretty gift bag.

  • Outline and flood icing with your finger
  • Wet on wet hearts you pull yourself
  • Six cookie shapes and many icing colors
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
