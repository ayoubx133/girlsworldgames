About the game

Drag the everyday items from the tray into the bag organizer and fit them all in without gaps. Tap a piece to rotate it into the spot you need. Every bag has a perfect solution, from a tiny purse to a big suitcase. When it is all packed, slide the zipper across to close it.

  • Handbags, backpacks, suitcases, totes and more
  • Block fitting puzzles that grow slowly
  • Six linings and zipper charms to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
