Flower Shop Florist Story

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Wrap beautiful bouquets for every customer in your flower shop!

About the game

Customers bring a card that shows how many roses, daisies, tulips or sunflowers they want, plus the wrap and ribbon colors. Choose a wrapping paper, then drag flowers into the bouquet exactly where you want them, or tap to add them. Tuck in soft greenery, tie a ribbon and add a little note. Press SERVE to see the order checked and earn stars and coins for new wraps.

  • Twelve hand drawn flowers and greens to arrange
  • Orders with counting, colors and ribbons
  • Polka dot, heart, star, stripe and gold lace wraps
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
