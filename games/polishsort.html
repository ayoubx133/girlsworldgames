Pour colors between bottles until every bottle holds one shade!

About the game

Tap a bottle to pop its cap, then tap another bottle to pour the top color over. A color may only land on the same color or in an empty bottle. Fill every bottle with a single shade and watch its cap twist back on with a sparkle. Use undo, a hint or an extra bottle when you are stuck, and spend coins on cute bottle styles.

  • Over 100 calm sorting levels that ramp up gently
  • Mystery bottles with hidden colors
  • Six bottle styles to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
