Paint butterflies, kitties and unicorns on happy kids!

About the game

Kids line up at your painting booth holding a card with the design they want. Trace the dotted outline with your brush, rub bright colors inside the lines, shake on glitter and stick shiny gems. The photo booth then snaps a before and after picture of the smiling kid.

  • Butterfly, kitty, rainbow, unicorn and more designs
  • Trace, color, glitter and gems by finger
  • Six magic brushes to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
