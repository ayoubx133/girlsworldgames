Draw My Dress Fashion Studio

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Draw, paint and sew your own dress for the fashion show!

About the game

Trace the dress sketch with your pencil and see a white dress appear. Paint each part with your brush, rub cute prints like dots, hearts and stars onto the fabric and sew the seams along the stitch lines. Your model wears the new dress in the studio show while the judges raise their scores. Coins unlock magic pencils.

  • Six dress shapes to sketch, paint and sew
  • Brush painting that keeps folds and shading
  • Studio fashion show with photo and judges
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
