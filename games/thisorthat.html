About the game

Each round has a theme such as Beach Day, Snow Day, Party Time or Princess Tea. Two cards pop up side by side: tap the one that suits the theme and it flies straight onto the doll. Choose hair, tops, skirts, shoes, bags, hats, sparkles and even the photo spot in up to eight speedy picks. Then strike a pose for the camera while the bunny, kitty and bear judges reveal their scores.

  • Speedy pick one choices that kids love
  • Eight fun themes with painted photo spots
  • Mix tops, skirts, shoes, bags, hats and hair
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
