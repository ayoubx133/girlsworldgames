Ribbon Bow Craft Shop

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Fold, pinch and decorate handmade ribbon hair bows!

About the game

Open a little bow shop and make hair bows by hand. Choose the ribbon your customer wants, pull it from the roll and cut it, drag both ends to the middle to form the loops, then press and hold to pinch the knot. Decorate with pearls, hearts, stars and diamonds and place the bow on the model hairstyle for a photo. Satin, polka dot, glitter, lace and velvet ribbons are all waiting.

  • Make bows step by step with your finger
  • Five ribbon styles and sparkly gems
  • Six hairstyles to style
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
