Spray paint, lace and sticker your dream sneakers!

About the game

Customers bring a card for the sneakers they dream about. Spray paint the toe, side, heel, tongue and sole, lace the shoe by tracing through the holes and flick bright paint splashes. Drag cute stickers like hearts, stars, kitties and rainbows onto the design and show it off on the stand. Happy customers pay coins that unlock new spray cans.

  • Spray paint every part of a high top
  • Lacing, splashes and twelve stickers
  • Kid customer orders with star ratings
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
