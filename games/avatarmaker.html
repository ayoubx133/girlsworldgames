About the game

Build your own anime portrait from the head to the shoulders. Choose a skin tone, shiny eyes, a happy smile, a hairstyle and any hair color, then add cat ears, glasses, flowers or a bunny hood and pick a background color and pattern. Every level gives a fun request card like Cat Lover With Glasses, and a good match earns up to three stars. Every portrait is kept in your personal album.

  • Mix hair, hair colors, eyes, smiles and cute extras
  • Request cards and your own portrait album
  • Dreamy music and photo frames to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
