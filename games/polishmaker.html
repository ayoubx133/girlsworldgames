Nail Color Mixing Factory

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Mix the perfect nail polish shade and paint it on!

About the game

Make your own polish in a dreamy factory. Spoon pigment powders into clear lacquer and watch the match dial as the colors swirl, then add shimmer, gold flakes or holo sparkles. Push the brush cap down, twist it closed and shake until the color is smooth. Paint the nails on the wheel with your finger and dry them under the lamp.

  • Pigment mixing with a match dial
  • Shimmer, flakes, glitter, stars and hearts
  • Six nail tip shapes to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
