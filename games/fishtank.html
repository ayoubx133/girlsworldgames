About the game

Build the aquarium you always wanted. First scrub the green algae off the glass until it shines. Then drag castles, coral, seaweed, shells, a treasure chest, a tiny diver and more onto the sand to match the tank wish. Tap the fish bags to release clownfish, goldfish, bettas, a sea turtle, a seahorse, a pufferfish and more, then shake in food until everyone is full and take a photo. Each order adds a friend to your fish book, and coins unlock six water themes from pink lagoon to starry night.

  • Clean, decorate, add fish and feed
  • Twelve sea friends for your fish book
  • Six water themes and twelve decorations
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
