Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Dress a magical hero, power up and turn gloomy clouds happy!

About the game

Each level has a magic card with wishes like a moon outfit, a heart wand or angel wings. Dress your hero with glittery outfits, wands, wings, crowns and hairstyles, then press MAGIC. Tap again and again to charge the power and watch her spin into her magical look. Then tap the grumpy rain clouds so her wand fires sparkles that turn them into smiling clouds with rainbows.

  • Six magical outfits plus wands, wings, crowns and hair
  • Tap to transform in a spinning sparkle show
  • Turn grumpy clouds into rainbows
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
