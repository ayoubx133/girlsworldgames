Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Mix colors, glitter and fruity flavors into shiny lip gloss!

About the game

Run a sweet beauty factory and make gloss for every customer. Pour the clear base, drip in colors and stir until the shade matches the order, then add glitter and a fruity flavor. Fill the tube to the line, snap on a charm cap, add a sticker and try the gloss on a big cute pout. Coins unlock new tube shapes.

  • Color mixing with a shade match meter
  • Glitter, fruit flavors, charm caps and stickers
  • Six tube shapes to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
