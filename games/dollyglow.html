Fresh Face Full Makeover

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Give every client a fresh face: spa, makeup, hair and a photo!

About the game

Each client arrives with a little order card. Lather her face with bubbly foam, wipe it clean, pop the spots, paint on a green mask and peel it away, then place cool cucumbers on her eyes. Choose shades and brush eyeshadow, blush and lipstick on with your finger, sprinkle glitter stars and finish with a new hairstyle and outfit. Take her picture at the vanity and collect stars for every wish you match.

  • Spa care and makeup painted with your finger
  • New outfits and hairstyles for each client
  • Six bright spa towels to collect
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
