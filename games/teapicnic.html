Afternoon Tea Garden Party

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Brew tea, cut sandwiches and set a picnic for animal friends!

About the game

Choose a tea, pour hot water into the glass teapot and stir until the color looks just right. Cut heart, star and bunny sandwiches with cookie cutters and spread jam on warm scones. Drag the tea set onto the picnic blanket and pour a cup for each guest. Then watch your animal friends hop in for the party!

  • Brew, cut, spread and pour by finger
  • Set the picnic blanket your way
  • Animal friends arrive for tea time
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
