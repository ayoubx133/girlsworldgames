Glam Tiles Match Game

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Match three beauty tiles at a time and clear the whole board!

About the game

Tap any tile that is not covered and it slides into the tray below. Collect three of the same beauty icon, like a lipstick, perfume or crown, and they vanish. The tray only holds seven tiles, so think ahead and clear the entire board. Undo, hint and shuffle are there whenever you need them.

  • Layered boards full of cute beauty icons
  • Every board can be cleared, with helpers
  • Six tile styles to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
