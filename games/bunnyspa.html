Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Clean, wash and fluff up adorable muddy bunnies!

About the game

A muddy bunny hops in with burrs and leaves stuck in its fur. Pull them out with your finger, scrub with a bubbly sponge and watch the fur change exactly where you rub, then rinse with the shower and towel off the drops. Blow dry until it is extra fluffy, brush it shiny, clip on the bow from the wish card and offer a carrot, berry, lettuce or apple. Make every bunny happy and unlock colorful bubble foams.

  • Bunny grooming with real before and after changes
  • Pull burrs, scrub, rinse, towel, dry and brush
  • Bows, treats and six bubble foam colors
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
