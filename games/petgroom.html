Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Bathe, dry and dress up muddy puppies and kittens!

About the game

A messy puppy or kitten visits your salon with an order from its owner. Rub in bubbles, spray them off, towel the fur dry, brush out the knots and blow dry until it is super fluffy. Then put on the bows, crowns, collars, raincoats and tutus from the order and watch your pet wiggle and send hearts. Coins unlock new tubs and cushions.

  • Finger grooming: foam, rinse, towel, brush and blow dry
  • An anime puppy and kitten that react to every step
  • Bows, crowns, collars, a raincoat and a tutu
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
