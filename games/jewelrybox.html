About the game

Read each order and choose gold, silver or rose metal for a necklace, ring, earrings, bracelet, tiara or choker. Tap or drag the right gems into the empty settings, tap to close the prongs and add a cute charm. Rub the piece with a polishing cloth until it shines, then place it in a gift box for the reveal. Every piece fills your jewelry collection, and coins unlock new box designs.

  • Set shiny gems into necklaces, rings and tiaras
  • Polish, lock and gift wrap every piece
  • Grow your own jewelry collection
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
