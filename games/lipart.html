Lipstick Makeup Artist

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Paint dreamy lip art with ombre, stencils and glitter!

About the game

Each customer brings a look card showing the lips she wants. Choose a lipstick shade and paint with your finger, blend a soft ombre in the middle, rub color over a heart or star stencil and lift it to reveal the shape. Shake on glitter, add shiny lip gems and swipe on a glossy top coat, then compare before and after in the magic mirror.

  • Finger painted lip art with stencils, ombre and glitter
  • Lots of look cards and stars to collect
  • Six sparkly vanity mirrors to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
