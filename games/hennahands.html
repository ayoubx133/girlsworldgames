About the game

Guests visit your henna corner in a sunny courtyard with a card of the design they like. Choose it in the pattern book, pick the right cone and trace dotted flowers, vines, paisleys and mandalas on the palm and fingers. Add dots and gems, fan the paste dry, then rub it off to uncover the warm orange and brown design.

  • Trace mandalas, paisleys, flowers and vines
  • Dry and peel the paste for a magic reveal
  • Many guest cards and six decorated cones
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
