About the game

Each client brings a spa menu with her steps and wishes, like a lilac peel mask, golden eye patches or star face gems. Wipe her face with a cotton pad, hold the warm steamer, scrub in gentle circles, then paint on a mask and peel it off slowly. Dab in moisturizer, slide the eye patches into place and press on shiny gems. Calm music and soft sounds make every visit cozy.

  • Seven soothing spa steps done by finger
  • Peel off masks, eye patches and sparkly gems
  • Calm music and six spa moods to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
