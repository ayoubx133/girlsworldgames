About the game

Tonight the palace throws a gala and every party comes with its own wish card: maybe a blue gown, curly hair or a snowy tiara. Tap or drag hairstyles, gowns, tiaras, jewels, shoes and a fan onto your princess until every wish is ticked. Press DONE for a photo in the grand ballroom while a bunny, a kitty and a bear raise their score cards. Coins open new halls like the Ice Palace, the Garden and the Starry Hall.

  • Two dozen sparkly anime gowns and hairstyles with tiaras, jewels and shoes
  • Gala wish cards and three adorable animal judges
  • A painted ballroom plus five more halls to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
