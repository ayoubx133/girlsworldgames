Cute Coffee Shop Decor

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Decorate a cute coffee shop and serve treats to your guests!

About the game

Furnish an empty cafe with tables, booth sofas, a counter, cake cases, wall art, lamps and plants to match the theme card, and choose their colors. Then open the doors: guests sit down and order lattes, tea, cakes, cupcakes, macarons and donuts. Tap the treat they want to serve it and collect tips. Spend coins on new decor and cafe colors.

  • Decorate your own pastel coffee shop
  • Serve drinks and sweets to cute guests
  • Earn tips to unlock more decor
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
