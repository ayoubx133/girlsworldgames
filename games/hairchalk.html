Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Rub pastel colors onto every strand for rainbow hair!

About the game

Your client brings a photo of the colorful hair she dreams about. Comb her long hair into strands, pick a chalk color and rub it along each one, from full color to tips only and soft ombre. Set the color with hair spray and add glitter if she wants it. The closer you match the photo, the more stars you collect!

  • Rub color onto every strand
  • Full, tips, ombre and rainbow looks
  • Six palettes from pastel to neon
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
