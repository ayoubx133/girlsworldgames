About the game

Each day the chalkboard shows a theme like Sports Day, Art Class or Picture Day, plus a short to do list. Tap or drag hairstyles, uniforms, shoes, backpacks and hair clips onto the student. Press DONE, smile for the class picture and watch the report card fill with A+ stamps. Coins unlock classroom moods like Sakura, Sunset, Snow Day and a Starry night.

  • Nine school looks from sailor and blazer to gym and art smock
  • A new chalkboard theme and to do list every day
  • Class photos and report cards full of A+ stamps
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
