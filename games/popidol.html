About the game

Every concert has a stage card with wishes like a pink outfit, twin tails or a golden microphone. Choose shiny stage outfits, hairstyles, mics, glow sticks, a headset, heart shades and star clips for your singer. Press SHOW and she dances under the lights while hearts rain down with the music; tap the pads on the beat to fill the fan meter and chain big combos. Great looks plus great timing win up to three stars.

  • Six shiny stage outfits and six hairstyles
  • Tap on the beat while the crowd cheers
  • Six light shows to open: hearts, stars, notes and more
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
