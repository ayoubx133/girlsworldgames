Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Collect hair locks and run with the longest hair ever!

About the game

Swipe left and right to change lanes on the candy road and grab silky locks so your hair grows longer and longer. Dodge snipping scissors and sticky gum, and run through shower arches for a boost. At the finish line the tape measure turns your hair length into bonus coins. Then pick a cute style: twin tails, a braid or a ponytail!

  • Hair that really grows while you run
  • Scissors, gum and shower arches
  • Six runners with different hair colors
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
