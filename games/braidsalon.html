Swipe to the beat and braid gorgeous hairstyles!

About the game

Your client sits in the chair and shows the braid she wants. Brush out the tangles, tap the sparkles to split the hair into strands, then swipe left and right with the beat to cross them and watch the braid grow. Make classic, fishtail, double and crown braids, tie a ribbon in her favorite color and add pearls, flowers or stars. Take a photo and count your stars!

  • Braiding with left and right swipes
  • Classic, fishtail, double and crown braids
  • Ribbons, pearls, flowers and six hair colors
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
