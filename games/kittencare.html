Feed, bathe, play with and cuddle your fluffy kittens!

About the game

Your kittens need you every day: keep an eye on the bars for food, bath, play, nap and love. Tap the bowl to feed, rub in bubbles and rinse, towel the fur dry, drag the yarn or wave the feather so your kitten pounces and brush the fur for happy purrs. Pet your kitten to sleep in the cozy bed and fill every bar to finish the day. A new kitten joins every few days, and bows, collars and crowns unlock with coins.

  • Five needs bars filled with finger care
  • Six cute kittens to adopt
  • Bows, collars, flowers and crowns
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
