Bubble Tea Maker Shop

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Shake up yummy bubble tea for every customer in your cafe!

About the game

Check the customer order, pick a cup and hold to pour tea and milk exactly to the line. Tap to add pearls, jelly, berries and ice, swirl whipped cream on top with your finger, press the lid until it clicks and push in a straw. Hand over the drink, see the happy smile and earn up to three stars for a perfect match. Spend your coins on cute cup stickers.

  • Hold to pour, swirl cream and shake every drink
  • Six cups, six tea flavors and four toppings
  • Chill cafe music and satisfying sounds
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
