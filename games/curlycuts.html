Hair Stylist Dream Salon

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Wash, cut, color and curl in your own dream hair salon!

About the game

Clients come in with an order card, for example a short cut, pink color, curls and a bow clip. Work shampoo foam into the hair and spray it out, comb away the knots, then sweep the scissors across to trim it to the right length. Brush on bright hair dye, twist curls with the curling iron or blow dry it sleek, and stick sparkly clips wherever you like. Take a salon photo, earn stars and coins and open new capes.

  • Cut, wash, comb, dye and curl with one finger
  • Six dye colors, curls or sleek hair and cute clips
  • Client orders, star ratings and six capes to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
