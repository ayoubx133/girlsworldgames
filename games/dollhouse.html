Decorate a tiny house room by room and meet the family!

About the game

Zoom into each room of a big open dollhouse, brush off the cobwebs and wipe the dust. Drag sofas, beds, tables, lamps and plants in to match the wish card and choose your favorite colors. When a room is done a new chibi friend moves in with a short sweet story. Finish all six rooms, then start a fresh house in a new color.

  • Six rooms in every house
  • Chibi friends move in with little stories
  • Lots of furniture and colors
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
