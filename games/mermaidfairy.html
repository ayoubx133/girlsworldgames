Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Switch between mermaid tails and fairy wings and style both!

About the game

Every level asks for a mermaid under the sea or a fairy in the flower forest, plus a few wishes like a gold tail or pink wings. Tap the big switch to transform her with a magic swirl, then choose hairstyles, shiny tails, shell tops, petal dresses, wings, crowns, pearls and wands. Press DONE for a photo while the bunny, kitty and bear judges reveal their scores. Coins open magic hours like Sunset, Moonlight and Rainbow.

  • Two magic looks in one game: tails and wings
  • Shiny tails, shell tops, petal dresses and wings
  • A sea palace and a flower forest with bubbles and fireflies
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
