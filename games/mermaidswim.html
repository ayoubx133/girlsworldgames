Swim through the sea, collect pearls and dodge jellyfish!

About the game

Drag up and down to guide your mermaid through the shimmering sea. Follow the pearl trails, grab golden rings for a speed boost and swim around jellyfish, fishing nets and grumpy pufferfish. Reach the giant clam at the end of every swim. Pearls become coins that unlock rainbow, coral, violet and golden tails.

  • Smooth one finger swimming
  • Pearls, boost rings and heart bubbles
  • Six colorful mermaid tails to unlock
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
