About the game

Welcome to the friendliest doggy hotel in town! Each puppy shows a little bubble with what it wants: food, water, a walk in the garden, playtime or a nap. Drag every puppy to the right spot before its bubble runs out and watch it munch, slurp, play and snooze. Happy puppies fill the happy meter for up to three stars. Days get busier with more puppies, nobody ever loses, and coins dress your pups in collars, bows, bandanas, flowers, bow ties and crowns.

  • Drag and drop care with five different needs
  • Up to four puppies at once
  • Collars, bows, bandanas and crowns
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
