Purse Maker Boutique

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Dip dye, paint and decorate stylish purses for your shop!

About the game

Customers visit your boutique with a card for their dream bag. Pick the shape, dip the white bag into a dye vat and dip again in another color for a pretty ombre. Paint the handles, snap on a gold clasp and hang a cute charm like a bunny, a pompom or a cherry. Sell the bag at the counter to earn coins for new shop awnings.

  • Six shapes: tote, round, heart, backpack, clutch and bucket
  • Dip dye ombre effects and painted handles
  • Gold clasps, cute charms and happy customers
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
