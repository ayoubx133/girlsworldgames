Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Snip, fold and color cute paper outfits for your cut out doll!

About the game

Every level brings an order like Rainy Day, Tea Party or Snow Day. Pull paper clothes, hats, bags and shoes out of the closet and drop them on the doll, and watch the little white tabs fold over her shoulders. On art class days grab the crayons and color a blank paper dress however you like, and it stays in the closet as your own design. Snap a picture on the craft table and see the animal judges hold up their scores.

  • Paper clothes with folding tabs, just like the real thing
  • Color your own paper dress with crayons
  • Six patterned paper backgrounds to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
