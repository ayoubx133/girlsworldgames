Dream Room Clean and Decor

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Clean up a messy bedroom and decorate it your way!

About the game

Toss the trash, rub off the dust and wipe away the cobwebs until the room shines. Then drag beds, lamps, rugs, plants and posters into place to match the theme card. Tap any piece to recolor it, flip it or make it bigger, and pick a pretty wallpaper. Each finished room gets a before and after photo plus stars and coins for new room colors.

  • Clean up trash, dust and cobwebs
  • 24 cute furniture pieces in many colors
  • A before and after photo for every room
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
