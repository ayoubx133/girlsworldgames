Build a magical fairy village that glows at night!

About the game

Pull weeds, pick up twigs and sweep the dry leaves to clear the forest glade. Then place mushroom houses, flower beds, a pond, stepping stones, lanterns, a bridge, swings and fairy lights to match the theme card, and choose their colors. Tap the night button to see windows and lights glow while fireflies dance. A little pixie flies along, and coins unlock new pixie friends.

  • Build cute fairy houses and gardens
  • Day and night with glowing lights and fireflies
  • Collect six pixie friends
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
