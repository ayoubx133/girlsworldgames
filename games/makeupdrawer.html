About the game

Drag a lipstick, blush, perfume or brush into another drawer. Group three matching items in one drawer and they pop away in a sparkle. More makeup waits behind and slides forward when the front row is empty. Clear every drawer quickly to earn three stars, with undo and hints whenever you need help.

  • Hundreds of cute cosmetics to sort
  • Always solvable levels with a gentle ramp
  • Six drawer colors to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
