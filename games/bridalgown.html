Wedding Dress Design Day

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Design a fairy tale wedding gown and walk down the aisle!

About the game

Each bride brings a card for the gown of her dreams. Pick the gown shape, brush a soft pastel tint over the white dress, stitch lace along the hem by following the dots and tap pearls onto the top. Choose the veil, a pearl tiara or flower crown, a bouquet and heels. Then the bride walks down the garden aisle under falling petals for a sweet photo at the flower arch.

  • Five gowns with pastel tints, lace and pearls
  • Veils, tiaras, flower crowns, bouquets and heels
  • A garden aisle walk with petals and a photo
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
