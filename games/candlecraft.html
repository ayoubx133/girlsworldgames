Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Melt, color, scent and pour cozy candles for your customers!

About the game

Read the order and choose a jar, teacup, pot, shell, star tin or tall glass. Drop wax flakes in the pot and hold to melt them, add a color chip and stir in circles, then mix in a sweet scent. Set the wick in the middle and hold to pour the wax to the line, with two colored layers on later orders. Sprinkle dried flowers on top, light a match and enjoy the glow, then earn coins for new flame colors.

  • Melt, stir and pour wax
  • Six cute containers and layered candles
  • Light it up for a warm glowing photo
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
