Pour, swirl, cut and wrap handmade marble soap bars!

About the game

Make soap by hand in a cozy workshop. Melt the base, tint each cup and drizzle the colors over the loaf wherever your finger moves. Drag the swirl stick to create marble patterns, let the loaf cool and swipe the knife to slice it into bars. Stamp every bar and tie it with a ribbon for your customer.

  • Marble swirls drawn by your finger
  • Cut, stamp and wrap the bars
  • Six loaf molds to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
