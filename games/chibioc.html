About the game

Make an original mini character from head to toe. Pick a skin tone, hairstyle and hair color, then dress her in a maid dress, magical outfit, kimono, comfy hoodie, bunny suit or sailor uniform. Add cute extras and a pet friend like a bunny, kitty, puppy, panda, dragon or fairy, choose a pose and a place, and match each request card for up to three stars. All your characters stay in your album.

  • Six outfits, six pets and lots of hair colors and extras
  • Poses like hop, sway, twirl and cheer
  • An album of every character you make
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
