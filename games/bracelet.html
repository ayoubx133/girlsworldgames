About the game

Read the pattern card from your friend, pick a cord and tap or drag beads onto it in the right order. Spell LOVE or BFF with letter beads, add kitty, flower and pearl beads, then pull the cord around and tap to tie the knot. Hang a sparkly charm, give it as a gift and see your friend wear it in a sweet photo. Coins unlock glossy, pearly, glitter, candy, crystal and jelly beads.

  • Bead patterns with satisfying click sounds
  • Letter beads, kitty beads and shiny charms
  • Six bead styles to collect
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
