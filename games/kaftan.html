Moroccan Dress Embroidery

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Color, embroider and style elegant dresses for celebrations!

About the game

Every level brings a celebration card with a wish list. Roll a fabric bolt across the white caftan to give it a lovely color, then follow the dotted lines with golden thread and watch the shiny embroidery appear. Drag a belt onto the waist, choose pretty jewels and pose for a photo in a glowing hall full of lanterns and roses. Coins unlock new golden needles.

  • Six elegant caftan styles in eight fabric colors
  • Golden thread tracing with a shiny reveal
  • Belts, jewels and a celebration photo
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
