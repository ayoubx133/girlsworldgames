About the game

Stroll through a flower greenhouse and make perfume for your friends. Tap roses, lavender, lilies and fruits to pick them, grind the petals and warm them into pure essence. Pour each scent to its line in a pretty bottle, tie a silk bow and add sparkling gems. Squeeze the spray bulb and release a cloud of petals and sparkles.

  • Pick flowers and fruits in a garden
  • Mix up to three scents in the right amounts
  • Six fancy bottles to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
