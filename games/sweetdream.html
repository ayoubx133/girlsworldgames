About the game

Each customer brings a photo of the sweet treat they want. Choose a cake, cupcake, donut, macaron, pudding or ice cream, pipe frosting with your finger, shake sprinkles anywhere and add toppings and candles. Serve it up and earn as many as three stars for matching the photo. Coins unlock lovely new cake stands.

  • Finger frosting and sprinkles anywhere you like
  • Six desserts, six frosting colors and many toppings
  • Sweet music box tunes in a pink bakery
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
