Sparkle Body Art Salon

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Make sparkly temporary tattoos with stencils, glue and glitter!

About the game

Customers bring a card for the glitter tattoo they want. Smooth the stencil onto the arm, brush glue into the holes and shake on glitter in the right colors. Dust away the loose sparkles, peel the stencil to see the design, add shiny gems and spray a sealing mist.

  • Butterflies, hearts, unicorns, rainbows and more
  • Stencil, glue, glitter and peel steps
  • Six stencil sheet colors to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
