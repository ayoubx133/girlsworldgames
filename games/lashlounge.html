Eyelash Beauty Salon

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Place lash extensions one by one and shape perfect brows!

About the game

Every client shows a card with the lash style and colors she wants. Swipe the old lashes away, choose natural, doll, cat eye or wispy lashes and set each extension with tweezers on the glowing dots. Curl them up, pluck the stray brow hairs, brush on brow color and dreamy eyeshadow. Then reveal the before and after in the hand mirror.

  • Lash by lash placement with tweezers
  • Brow shaping, brow tint and eyeshadow
  • Plenty of client cards and six tweezer sets
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
