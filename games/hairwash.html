About the game

A sleepy client leans back at the wash basin, ready to relax. Spray warm water over her hair, choose the shampoo scent she asked for and rub in fluffy foam, then rinse it away. Smooth in conditioner, pat dry with a soft towel, blow dry with warm air and comb it shiny. Finish with a cute clip while calm rain music and bubbly sounds play.

  • Relaxing wash, foam, rinse and blow dry
  • Scented shampoos and cute clips
  • Six cozy spa rooms to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
