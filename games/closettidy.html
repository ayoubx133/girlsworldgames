Fold, hang and pair clothes to make every closet neat!

About the game

The clothes are scattered all over the floor and it is time to organize. Drag each dress or hoodie onto the hanger with the matching bow, lay crumpled tees on the mat and swipe to fold them, then slide them into their cubby. Drop a sneaker or sock onto its twin to make a pair until everything has its place. Every finished closet shows a before and after photo.

  • Relaxing folding, hanging and pairing
  • A before and after photo for each closet
  • Six closet colors to open
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
