Dress your ballerina and dance to gentle ballet music!

About the game

Choose a tutu, ribbon pointe shoes, a hairstyle, a crown and a wand to match the show card. When the curtain rises a soft ballet waltz begins: tap the hearts on the ring, hold the ribbons and tap the stars to make her spin. The bunny, kitty and bear judges show their scores at the end. Coins unlock new stage colors.

  • Six sparkly tutus with matching shoes
  • Easy tap, hold and spin rhythm dancing
  • Soft original ballet music and animal judges
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no data collection. Read our privacy policy.
