Shiny Mold Keyring Craft

SimulationFreeOfflineNo ads

Coming soon to the Amazon Appstore for Fire tablets

Pour glossy resin into molds and make sparkly keyrings!

About the game

Read the order and choose a heart, star, moon, kitty, flower or butterfly mold. Pick a resin color and hold to pour to the line, add a second layer and swirl it into marble with your finger. Shake in glitter and drop in tiny flowers, stars or hearts, hold the UV lamp until it turns glossy and pop the charm out. Clip on a keyring for a photo and earn coins for new keyring styles.

  • Hold to pour and swirl glossy resin
  • Glitter, flowers, stars and hearts inside
  • Six molds and six keyring styles
  • Earn coins and unlock adorable extras
  • Made for Fire tablets, old and new
  • Plays fully offline
