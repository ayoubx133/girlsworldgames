Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Girls World Games ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you or your child play any game published by Girls World Games (each a "Game") on the Amazon Appstore or any other store. This one policy covers all of our Games.

1. In short

  • Our Games do not collect, store or share personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no sign up, no email, no chat and no social features.
  • There are no ads, no advertising identifiers and no third party tracking or analytics tools.
  • Progress, coins, unlocked items and settings stay on your device only.
  • We never sell or rent any data.

2. Information kept on your device

Game progress such as levels, coins, unlocked items and sound settings is saved only inside the Game's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we run no game servers. Uninstalling the Game removes it.

3. Children's privacy (COPPA)

Our Games are made to be suitable for everyone, including young children. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws such as the GDPR rules for children. Because our Games collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, no parental consent is needed to play. Our Games contain no ads, no links to social networks and no way for a child to share information.

4. Advertising and analytics

Our Games contain no advertising, no advertising SDKs and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not use advertising identifiers or device identifiers.

5. Purchases

Coins and unlockable items in our Games are earned by playing and can never be bought with real money. Our Games are free to download. If a store processes any transaction, it does so under its own policies and we never receive payment details, names or store account information.

6. Permissions

Our Games ask for no sensitive permissions. They do not access contacts, photos, the microphone, the camera, location or the internet.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access or delete. Parents with any question are welcome to contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

Girls World Games. Questions about privacy or anything else: support@girlsworldgames.com.
